Privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Critiq is a personal project by Muhammad Rayan Asad. It keeps a log of what you watch and uses it to suggest what to watch next. This page explains what it stores and why.

What Critiq stores

  • Your name and email address from Google, when you sign in. Critiq never sees your Google password.
  • The ratings, reviews and watchlist you add.
  • Your preferences, such as favourite actors and directors, genres and streaming services.

How it's used

Only to show you your own library and to make your recommendations. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for tracking. If you send a movie-night invite, the friend who opens it gets picks blended from both of your libraries.

Where it's kept

Your data is stored in a Postgres database hosted by Neon. The app runs on Streamlit Community Cloud. Movie details come from TMDB and scores from OMDb; those services receive the titles Critiq looks up, never your personal information.

Deleting your data

You can remove any rating in the app. To delete everything, including your account, email mrayan_asad@hotmail.com from the address you signed in with, and it will be deleted within 30 days.
