# Critiq landing page: copy

Voice: the app's own (a film critic's notebook, plain and film-literate)
Pairs: exact, not cold; warm about films, dry about the machinery
Person: "you" for what the visitor gets; Critiq is the subject in feature lines
Sentences: mostly 8 to 16 words; one longer one where the mechanism is explained
Humour: none in the hero, the FAQ or the close
House words: rate, review, pick, watch, movie night (the app's own words, the same every time)
Never: virtue words about Critiq, a pun on the name, invented users or figures

## How picks work

## Every pick comes with its reason

Critiq ranks titles by how close they sit to what you rated highly, then shows why: the film you rated, the director, the cast or the words you wrote.

## Reviews

## Your written reviews change the picks

Critiq removes the opinion words from each review first. What's left, like "twist ending" or "alien language", is matched against plots, themes and keywords.

Low ratings count too: they push similar titles away.
Ratings run from 0 to 5 in quarter stars.

## Features

## Picks that fit tonight. Your mood, your services and the critics' scores.

### Finds a film for your mood
Tap Something mind-bending or Make me laugh, or type your own, like a slow-burn crime thriller.

### Rates in quarter stars
Slide from 0 to 5 in steps of a quarter. A low rating pushes similar titles down.

### Hides what you can't stream
Turn on Netflix, Crave or Prime Video, and Discover shows what's on them in Canada.

### Pulls scores from four sources
IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes scores from OMDb, cast and posters from TMDB, where to watch from JustWatch.

## Movie night

## Movie night for two people

Critiq blends your ratings with a friend's and ranks titles by the lower of your two matches, so a film one of you would hate can't win.

## Import

## Import your Letterboxd history in 3 steps

### Export from Letterboxd
Go to Settings, Data, Export your data. You get one zip file.

### Upload the zip to Critiq
Open the Account menu and drop it in. Ratings, reviews and your watchlist come across.

### Get your first picks
Recommendations arrive with reasons taken from the reviews you wrote on Letterboxd.

## Questions

### Is Critiq free?
Yes. There are no paid plans.

### What do I need to sign in?
A Google account. Your ratings are saved to it, so they follow you to any device.

### Which country is the streaming information for?
Canada. Stream, rent and buy options come from JustWatch through TMDB.

### Do I need a Letterboxd account?
No. You can rate titles one at a time in Critiq. The import only saves typing.

### How does movie night choose?
Each title gets a match for each of you, and Critiq ranks by the lower of the two.

### Where do posters and scores come from?
Posters, cast and dates from TMDB. IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes scores from OMDb.
